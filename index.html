One deployment serves many schools, each on its own subdomain with its own branding, roles, data and billing. It covers admissions, attendance, timetabling, homework, exams and report cards, fee collection and parent communication. Lighter modes support solo tutors and field-service contractors.

  • Realtime notifications through ActionCable, with Web Push for closed PWAs.
  • AI lesson plans, homework grading with per-student feedback, and class insights. Every AI output passes automated checks and a teacher’s review before students see it.

Positive News Daily

A news reader that only carries good news

Background jobs scrape news portals on a schedule, an LLM decides which stories are genuinely uplifting, and another pass boils each one down to five key points so readers can skip the long version. Stories come from India and around the world in over a dozen languages, on the web and in an Android app.

  • Scraping and summarising run as separate queued jobs, with daily tracking of AI token use and cost.
  • Classification falls back from DeepSeek to Claude to an offline model, so the feed keeps moving through an outage. A multilingual headline prefilter skips the paid call for obvious rejects.
  • Subscriptions through Razorpay and Google Play, rewarded ads, comments with moderation, and weekly digests.

दासबोध Dasbodh

Offline reader for the works of Samarth Ramdas Swami

All 200 samas and 7,751 ovis of the Dasbodh, plus Manache Shlok and Atmaram, in Devanagari. It has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it never needs the internet. The care went into the text itself: readings were compared word by word against printed and online sources, and changed only when at least two sources agreed.

  • Short Marathi meanings bundled offline, shown on long-press.
  • Read-aloud using the phone's own Marathi text-to-speech voice, with a spelling clean-up layer for the older text.
